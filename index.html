One bucket at a time

Range sessions and backyard net practice, about five days a week. A launch monitor sits behind the ball on every swing, so every video comes with the real numbers: carry, ball speed, launch and club speed.

  • 1Hit a bucket and work through the bag, wedges up to driver.
  • 2Pull the best swing with each club and put the numbers on screen.
  • 3Post it, then go hit another bucket. Repeat until scratch.
